---
title: "Rules"
---

1. **Be respectful.** Treat people the way you'd want to be treated. No slurs, harassment or spam. Swearing is fine.

2. **Help each other out.** A lot of people here are new. If someone asks for help or looks lost, point them in the right direction.

3. **No griefing or harassing other players.** This is a PvE server. Don't kill or hurt other players, and don't break, burn or mess with their stuff.

4. **Don't take what isn't yours.** That includes cars parked at someone's base and anything sitting outside their walls. If you're not sure, leave it.

5. **One safehouse per person.** Alts count as the same person. Visit it at least once every 7 days or you lose the claim, and the building resets.

6. **Non-residential bases need an admin's OK first.** No claiming gun stores, police stations or other big loot spots everyone needs.

7. **No cheats, duping or exploiting bugs.** Found a bug? Tell an admin, don't use it.

8. **If someone breaks a rule,** message an admin in game or on Discord. Screenshots help. Admins have the final say.

## Points

Breaking a rule gets you points. **10 points is a ban.**

| Points | For |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Minor stuff. Spam, being rude, claiming a spot you shouldn't. |
| 3-5 | Stealing, griefing, harassing someone. |
| 10 | Cheating, duping, exploits, hate speech. Straight to a ban. |

You'll get a warning in game or on Discord each time you get points. Points drop by 1 for every 2 weeks you go without getting new ones.

Banned? You can appeal once on Discord. Tell us what happened and why you should be let back in.
